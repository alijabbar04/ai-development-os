# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be accurate. Assume the person you are talking to is
competent and acting in good faith. Criticise work, not people.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and any
other project space.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be specific.** "This is wrong" is not useful. "This refuses on stock Windows
  because `C:\ProgramData` grants `BUILTIN\Users` write by default" is.
- **Separate the work from the author.** A defect in a change is a fact about the
  change.
- **Say what you do not know.** This project treats "I could not verify this
  read-only" as a valuable statement, not a weakness. Overstated confidence has
  caused more problems here than admitted uncertainty ever has.
- **Accept correction.** Having a claim disproved is the process working. Several
  of this project's strongest guarantees exist because a review found the previous
  version of them to be vacuous.
- **Give reasons.** A rule without its reason is a rule the next person deletes,
  and a review comment without its reason is one the author cannot act on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or demeaning remarks about anyone, including on the basis of
  race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation,
  disability, age, religion, or experience level.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, or persistently reopening a settled decision without new
  argument or evidence.
- **Deliberately misrepresenting what a change does**, including describing
  unverified work as verified, or removing a security assertion so that a change
  fits without saying so. In a project whose entire method is evidence, this is
  the most damaging thing a contributor can do, and it is a conduct matter rather
  than only a technical one.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainer, at the address on this repository's
commit history. Reports are handled confidentially. Because this is a
single-maintainer project, there is no separate committee — if your concern is
about the maintainer, you are entitled to say so publicly and to expect that a
substantive response addresses it.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and may block accounts, for
conduct that violates this document. Enforcement scales with severity and with
whether the behaviour continues after being named. Where a decision is made, the
reasoning will be given.

## Attribution

This is an original document rather than an adaptation of a standard template,
because the two project-specific clauses above — on admitted uncertainty and on
misrepresenting a change — are the ones that matter most here, and a template
would not contain them.
